e-HKD concept: both legs or neither

After an escrow release, the exporter's claim moves from Bank H to Bank O (leg A, simulated tokenised commercial-bank deposits) while Bank H compensates Bank O in simulated central-bank money (leg B, e-HKD-style wholesale mock). A two-phase-commit coordinator makes both legs complete together or neither completes.

Real multi-ledger atomicity, legal settlement finality, HKMA/EnsembleTX/mBridge connectivity and FX are not implemented. This is a conceptual extension, not a real e-HKD transaction.
